The Shared Platform (one stack for all seven)

Client / distribution Cloudflare (free, durable) Supabase (free tier) External / monetization
Fixed cost to launch on Android: $25 one-time (Play) + ~₹800/yr (domain). Cloudflare Pages serves unlimited traffic free; Workers gives 100k req/day; Supabase free tier needs a weekly keep-alive ping (a Workers cron does it). iOS starts as an installable PWA at $0 — pay Apple's $99/yr only after revenue justifies it.
