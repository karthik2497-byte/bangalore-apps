CardGuard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026
Short version: CardGuard has no accounts and no server holding your data. Your cards, bills and payment history live in your own device's browser storage. We never ask for, and the app cannot store, a full card number, CVV, PIN, expiry date, or your bank login.

1. What the app stores

CardGuard saves the following in your device's local storage (localStorage), on your device only:

  • Per card: the bank name, a nickname you choose, the last four digits only, the monthly due day, and a typical bill amount
  • Bills and payment history: due date, amount, amount paid, an optional reference/UTR you type in, and paid/pending status
  • Preferences: reminders on/off, how many days ahead to remind, notification sound
  • Which reminders have already been shown today, so you are not notified twice
  • Whether Premium has been unlocked on this device

A copy of the card list, reminder settings and due-date bookkeeping is also written to the browser's Cache Storage on your device. This is what lets the app's background worker remind you while the app is closed — it is the same data, in the same browser, and it is not transmitted anywhere.

2. What the app never stores

  • No full card number, CVV, PIN, expiry date, or cardholder name
  • No net-banking or bank app credentials
  • No name, email, phone number, or account of any kind
  • No location, contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies

Local storage on a phone is not encrypted by the app. It is protected by your device's own lock screen and by the browser's per-site isolation. Because the app deliberately holds nothing more identifying than a bank name and four digits, a lost device does not expose anything usable to make a payment. Use Settings → Clear All Data to erase everything, or uninstall the app / clear site data.

3. Reminders and notifications

Reminders are generated on your device by comparing today's date with the due days you entered. Nothing is sent to a server to decide when to notify you, and no push service is involved. Background reminders rely on a browser feature (periodic background sync) that is only available in some browsers when the app is installed to your home screen; the Settings screen tells you which case applies to you.

4. Gmail auto-detect (optional, advanced)

This feature is off unless you deliberately set it up, and it is not required to use the app. If you enable it:

  • You supply your own Google OAuth client ID. The app requests the read-only gmail.readonly permission through Google's own sign-in screen.
  • The resulting access token is held in this browser's memory only. It is not stored on a server, and it is not sent to us. Equally, there is no server protecting it — this is why the feature is labelled advanced/self-hosted.
  • Your browser queries the Gmail API directly for bill-related emails from Indian card issuers. The bank name, last four digits, amount and due date are extracted in your browser. Email bodies are never uploaded anywhere and are not saved by the app.
  • You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

CardGuard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Payments

CardGuard Premium is a one-off yearly purchase made through Google Play Billing inside the Play Store version of the app. Google processes the payment; the app receives only a confirmation that the purchase exists and records the unlock on your device. We never see your payment method. CardGuard does not take, hold, or move money towards your credit card bills — marking a bill as paid is a note to yourself, made after you pay through your own bank.

6. Network requests

The app works offline. It loads its own files from this website, a web font from Google Fonts, and Google's sign-in script — which only runs if you use the optional Gmail feature. Like any web request, the hosting provider records standard server log information such as IP address and timestamp, used for delivery and abuse prevention and not linked to any profile. No analytics or tracking SDK is included.

7. Export and import

Export writes a JSON backup file to your device through your browser's download mechanism. Where that file then goes is up to you — treat it as you would any personal document. Import replaces the app's current data with the contents of a file you choose.

8. Not a bank

CardGuard is an independent reminder tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI Card, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, IndusInd Bank, American Express, Citibank, RBL Bank, or any other issuer. Always confirm due dates and amounts against your issuer's own statement or app.

9. Children

CardGuard is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.
