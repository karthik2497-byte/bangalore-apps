Thraasu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026
Short version: Without a shop account, everything stays on the phone. A shop account keeps the shop's prices, photos, item list and stock on our server so every phone in the shop sees the same. We never collect anything about the shop's customers.

1. On the phone

The current bill, the shop name for receipts, the shop's prices and its photos are saved in the phone's browser storage. Without an account they never leave the phone; a backup file is made only when you tap Backup, and goes wherever you choose to send it.

2. What a shop account stores

  • Shop name and the owner's mobile number, given when asking for a shop. The number is used only to confirm who owns the shop (for example before resetting a forgotten PIN) and to contact the shop about its account and any fee.
  • The PIN, as a salted one-way hash (PBKDF2): it cannot be read back, by us or anyone.
  • The shop's prices, the product photos it adds, its item list and stock counts.
  • For each sold bill: which items and how much of each, to lower the stock. These are deleted after 60 days. No customer name, number or payment detail is ever collected.
  • A login record per phone (stored only as a hash), so the phone stays logged in.

This is stored with Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages and its D1 database). It is visible to the shop through its code and PIN, and to the operator of this service to run and support it. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

3. Network requests

The app loads its typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which receives your IP address and browser details under Google's privacy policy. With a shop account, the app talks to this same website to sync. Like any web request, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) records standard server logs such as IP address and time, used for delivery and abuse prevention.

4. Deleting your data

Logging out removes the shop's data from that phone. To delete the shop and everything stored for it on the server, contact us below; it is deleted in full.

5. Fees

A shop account is free for now (₹0 a month). Any fee will be shown in the app before it applies.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
